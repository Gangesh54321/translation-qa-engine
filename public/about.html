14 Categories of Precision

We've categorized our rules into 14 professional segments, giving you total control over your audit preferences.

  1. Terminology: Termbase matching, forbidden words, and inflection validation.
  2. Numbers: Numeric count, mismatch, and localization checks.
  3. Tags: XML/HTML tag integrity and order mismatch.
  4. Punctuation: Missing end marks, double punctuation, and quote mismatches.
  5. Whitespace: Leading/trailing spaces, double spaces, and NBSP validation.
  6. Capitalization: Sentence-start checks and All-Caps mismatch.
  7. Length: Expansion limits and suspiciously short translations.
  8. Consistency: Cross-segment repetition and identical source/different target checks.
  9. Formatting: Line break and paragraph mismatch.
  10. Language: Script mixing (Latn/Cyrl) and locale variant detection.
  11. Segment Structure: Untranslated segments and source-copy detection.
  12. Regex / Pattern: URL/Email mismatch and custom regex rule support.
  13. Localization: Date, currency, and address format validation.
  14. Style Guide: Tone mismatch and branding adherence.
